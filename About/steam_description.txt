[h1]Regions and Societies[/h1]
[b][url=https://github.com/Regions-and-societies/Core-MMF/wiki]Official Wiki and Documentation[/url][/b]
[b]Overhauls the world map with dynamic geographic province generation, world map overlays, and territory borders.[/b]

Regions and Societies is the continuation of Regions and Territories under a new identity. It divides the globe into geographic regions with defined borders, territorial ownership, a full demographic profile per region, and a living population that grows over time.

---

[h2]Best on a New Colony - Existing Saves Supported in Compatibility Mode[/h2]
Regions and Societies does most of its work during world generation: it draws the province boundaries and places faction settlements according to them. Added to a world already in progress, it adopts that world in [b]Compatibility Mode[/b] - provinces are drawn and territory is owned and shown, but placement stays with vanilla or whichever mod owns it. The mode is decided once per save, shown under [b]Strict territorial ownership[/b] in the mod settings.

[b]A new colony is still the recommended way to play[/b] - only a world generated with the mod installed gets geography-driven placement, occupancy limits, border buffers and sequential expansion. Removing the mod from an in-progress save is not supported and will log errors.

---

[h2]Key Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Geographic Domain Mapping:[/b] Splits the world into dynamic provinces with clear, terrain-aware boundaries.
[*] [b]Territorial Ownership:[/b] Factions own the ground they actually hold - a four-tier ladder from exclusive hold to loose claim, shaded on the map, with coastlines and mountains reading as real borders.
[*] [b]Geography-Driven Faction Placement:[/b] Nations settle as contiguous territory, weighing established claim against resources and shying away from a rival's ground - one settlement per region, with intrusions costing goodwill, and squaring their domains off rather than spidering.
[*] [b]Regional Demographics:[/b] Every region carries a deterministic demographic profile across seven axes - age structure, sex ratio, xenotype castes, ideoligions, wealth, education and employment - derived from the factions pressing on it, their tech and beliefs, and the land itself. Nothing is stored in your save.
[*] [b]Demographic Map Overlays:[/b] Seven world-map overlays, one per axis, alongside faction territory and the population-density heatmap - all built on the Map Mode Framework. The expanded region panel shows the full breakdown.
[*] [b]Overlays Under One Menu:[/b] Every Regions and Societies overlay - territory, region borders, population density, natural barriers, residences and the seven demographic axes - lives under a single button on the world-view bar.
[*] [b]Hooks for Other Mods:[/b] War and drafting mods can skew a region's sex ratio (drafts in progress, combat losses with generational recovery); expansion mods can rank territory with the same compactness metric core uses.
[/list]

[h2]Changelog (v0.3.0) - Living Population Model[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]New region partitioning.[/b] Regions are drawn inside the terrain's natural sections - biome, mountains and coasts - then cut into even pieces, so borders follow the land. Every region is guaranteed to be a single connected landmass.
[*] [b]Pluggable partition algorithm.[/b] Pick how the world is cut from a dropdown in mod settings (contain-then-subdivide, or the legacy anchor-Voronoi look), and other mods can register their own. Each world remembers the algorithm and version it was generated with, so loading an old save never re-cuts its map.
[*] [b]Region inspection panel.[/b] A three-tab region window - Region (shape mini-map, ownership, features, resources, wildlife), Population (the seven demographic axes), and Economy (goods, housing, crises).
[*] [b]Faction character.[/b] A faction's education, wealth and standing now reflect what it is - raiders read as raiders, an imperial court as an elite.
[*] [b]Settlement growth[/b] from in-game birthrates, a [b]residence layer[/b] (dwellings modelled separately from population), and all map overlays consolidated under one menu button.
[*] [b]Performance.[/b] Region demographics are precomputed at load, so overlays open instantly, and the aggregation stays linear even at 100% coverage.
[*] [b]Odyssey multi-layer worlds[/b] - off-surface world objects (orbital habitats, space sites) are handled cleanly, so regions, demographics and placement stay correct with space factions on the orbit layer.
[*] [b]Now compatible with Layered Atmosphere and Orbit[/b] - the region-border and capital overlays are pinned to the surface layer, so LAO's world-render patch no longer null-references on them. No longer marked incompatible.
[*] [b]Fixed (thanks to community member 96localk):[/b] every generated faction was created hidden with no leader, and unselected factions could leak in. Factions now generate visible with leaders, only chosen factions appear, and a one-time repair fixes old saves.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility Matrix[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Royalty DLC:[/b] Compatible
[*] [b]Ideology DLC:[/b] Compatible
[*] [b]Biotech DLC:[/b] Compatible
[*] [b]Anomaly DLC:[/b] Compatible
[*] [b]Odyssey DLC:[/b] Fully Compatible and Optimized. Space factions are generated on the Orbit layer naturally, and terrestrial factions generate on the Surface layer.
[*] [b]Map Mode Framework:[/b] Required for the map mode overlays.
[*] [b]Realistic Planets 2 and the RP2 edition:[/b] Incompatible with this edition by design - only one map-mode framework may load. Use the Realistic Planets 2 edition instead.
[*] [b]Layered Atmosphere and Orbit (LAO):[/b] Compatible since 0.3.0 - the region-border and capital overlays are pinned to the surface layer.
[*] [b]Empire, World Domination, Vanilla Factions Expanded, Vanilla Outposts Expanded:[/b] Supported through dedicated companion compatibility patches, released separately. Core alone classifies vanilla world objects.
[*] [b]Existing saves:[/b] Supported in Compatibility Mode - provinces are generated and territory is drawn, but placement rules stand down and stay with vanilla or whichever mod owns them. A new colony still gets the full experience.
[/list]
